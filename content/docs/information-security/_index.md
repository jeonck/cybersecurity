Information security governance turns security principles — confidentiality, integrity, and availability — into repeatable operational practice: who can access what, how sensitive data is labeled and protected, how incidents and breaches are logged and disclosed, and how long records are kept before disposal. This category collects the working documents and templates that make those decisions auditable rather than ad hoc.

These artifacts are typically owned and maintained by the CISO, the security or GRC (governance, risk, and compliance) team, and delegated data/system owners, with periodic review by internal audit and legal or privacy counsel. Together they form the evidentiary backbone used in security audits, regulatory inquiries, and incident post-mortems.

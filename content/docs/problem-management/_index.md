Problem Management is the ITIL-aligned discipline that investigates the underlying causes of incidents, records them as problems, and drives them to permanent resolution rather than repeated workarounds. It sits downstream of incident management: where incident response restores service quickly, problem management asks why the disruption happened and prevents it from recurring, tracking known errors and their fixes until they are formally closed.

These records are typically owned and maintained by an IT service management (ITSM) function or a designated problem manager, working alongside security incident response, engineering, and operations teams who supply the technical detail needed for root-cause analysis. They form the evidence trail used in service reviews, change approvals, and continual service improvement, and they give security programs a structured place to convert a security incident's root cause into a tracked, resolvable problem.

## What this is

This knowledge base collects the templates, trackers, logs, and policy documents a security team reaches for on a regular basis, organised into **eight operational domains**:

1. [Information Security](/docs/information-security/) — access rights, data classification, breach notification, DLP, retention, KPIs
2. [Network Security](/docs/network-security/) — DDoS mitigation, IP allow/deny lists, access control logging, device inventory, traffic monitoring
3. [Cloud Security](/docs/cloud-security/) — access control, asset inventory, backup/recovery testing, incident logging, configuration baselines
4. [Application Security](/docs/application-security/) — patch tracking, secure coding, mobile app testing, misconfiguration logs, static analysis, threat modeling
5. [Security Management](/docs/security-management/) — acceptable use, password, backup, disposal, classification, transfer, and ISMS policies
6. [Incident Management](/docs/incident-management/) — incident policy/process, major and intern incident reports, structural and workplace incident reports
7. [Problem Management](/docs/problem-management/) — known error records, major problem reports, problem management process
8. [Disaster Recovery](/docs/disaster-recovery/) — DR approach, asset register, closure reports, communications plan, DR plan template

## How each page is structured

Every template page follows the same three-part structure so you can scan quickly:

- **I. Overview** — what the document is, and why a security program needs it
- **II. Structure & Process** — the fields/sections the document should contain and the workflow around it, shown as a diagram
- **III. Best Practices & Comparison** — maturity guidance and how the document relates to neighbouring templates or standards

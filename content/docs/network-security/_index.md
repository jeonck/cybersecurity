Network security covers the controls that protect data in motion and the infrastructure that carries it: perimeter and DDoS defense, segmentation and access control between zones, and continuous monitoring for anomalous traffic. This category collects the working documents that turn those controls into auditable, repeatable practice rather than one-off firewall changes.

These artifacts are typically owned and maintained by the network security engineering team, with day-to-day operation split between the Network Operations Center (NOC) and the Security Operations Center (SOC), and periodic review by the CISO's office during audits and incident post-mortems.

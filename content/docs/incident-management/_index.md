Incident management covers the full lifecycle of an unplanned event, from detection through triage, escalation, resolution, and post-incident reporting — spanning security incidents (intrusions, malware, data exposure) as well as physical and personnel incidents (workplace safety, facilities damage, conduct issues). This category collects the working documents that turn incident handling into a consistent, auditable process rather than ad hoc verbal handoffs.

These artifacts are typically owned by the SOC / incident response team for security-driven events, with facilities management and HR owning the physical-safety and personnel-related templates. The two groups interface whenever a non-security incident carries a data, device, or system-access dimension.

Application security covers the practices that keep custom-built and third-party software free of exploitable weaknesses across the software development lifecycle: secure coding standards, patch and dependency management, static and dynamic testing, and structured threat modeling. This category collects the working documents that turn those practices into auditable, repeatable process rather than one-off code reviews or ad hoc patch runs.

These artifacts are typically owned and maintained by the AppSec team, with day-to-day execution shared across engineering teams during development and release, and periodic review by the AppSec lead or CISO's office during audits and incident post-mortems.

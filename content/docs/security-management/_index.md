Security Management collects the governance-level policies that underpin an Information Security Management System (ISMS): acceptable use of assets, password requirements, backup and recovery, information classification and transfer, secure disposal and destruction, and the compliance program that ties these controls to legal and regulatory obligations. Together they translate high-level security principles into documented, auditable rules that every employee and system owner is expected to follow.

These policies are typically owned and maintained by the CISO and the security or GRC (governance, risk, and compliance) team, drafted in consultation with legal, HR, and IT operations, and formally approved through an ISMS steering committee or equivalent governance body. They form the policy backbone referenced during internal audits, external certification assessments (such as ISO/IEC 27001 or ISMS-P), and regulatory inquiries.

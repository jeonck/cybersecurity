Identity and Access Management (IAM) covers the protocols and mechanisms that establish who a user or system is (authentication) and what they're allowed to do (authorization), plus how that identity is federated across systems. This section collects the core IAM building blocks — protocols, standards, and models — referenced throughout the rest of this knowledge base.
